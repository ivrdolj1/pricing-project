FAQ

Who is Sezonar for?

Sezonar serves large seasonal resorts and campsites, small seasonal hotels, year-round city hotels, and seasonal restaurants and beach bars, particularly in Istria and Kvarner.

Can staff access schedules on their phones?

Yes. Employees can view their schedules and swap shifts on their mobile phones.

What does Hospitality Complete include?

Alongside shift scheduling, it provides mobile time clock functionality, overtime warnings and Croatian-compatible payroll export for hotels, resorts and campsites.

Do I pay for employees during the off-season?

You are charged only for employees with at least one scheduled shift in the billing month. If no employees are active, your employee-based charge is €0.00.
